From next tokens to sequence probability

Write the sequence as X = (x1, …, xT), where xi is the token at position i and T is the sequence length. The vertical bar means “given”: each probability uses the tokens before it as context.

The chain rule writes the probability of a whole sequence as a product of next-token probabilities, one for each prefix:

P(X)=∏t=1TP(xt∣x1,…,xt−1)

The first four tokens form the given prompt, x1, …, x4. Here we calculate the probability of the chosen continuation conditioned on that prompt, so the product starts at x5.
