Model assumptions (read before trusting a number)
  • World size W = TP × CP × PP × D. EP and F subdivide D; neither adds GPUs. Rank ↦ coordinates is a mixed-radix decomposition in the rank order shown; EP is the innermost part of the D coordinate.
  • CP ranks hold identical weights, so gradients reduce over D × CP ranks, and ZeRO/FSDP shards over F × CP. Expert gradients reduce over (D/EP) × CP and shard over gcd(F, D/EP) × CP.
  • Mixed-precision AdamW: bf16 weights (2 B) + bf16 gradients (2 B) + fp32 master, m, v (12 B). ZeRO-1 shards optimizer state, ZeRO-2 also gradients, ZeRO-3 (FSDP) also weights, with two gathered layers in flight.
  • Activations per layer follow Korthikanti et al. with sequence parallelism (divided by TP) and CP (sequence divided by CP); SwiGLU MLP; fp32 logits on the last stage. 1F1B keeps min(PP − stage, A) micro-batches in flight; AFAB keeps A. Plus 3 GB for CUDA context, NCCL, and allocator.
  • Compute at 55% of peak bf16 throughput. Full recomputation adds one forward pass (compute × 4/3) but MFU counts only model FLOPs 6·Nactive + 6·L·s·h per token.
  • Communication: ideal ring bytes, bandwidth of the slowest link a group uses (per-GPU NVLink/PCIe inside a node, per-GPU NIC across nodes), plus a latency per collective. TP (4 all-gathers + 4 reduce-scatters per layer), EP all-to-all, and PP sends are fully exposed. CP ring transfers hide behind attention compute. DP/ZeRO reductions hide behind the last backward pass; ZeRO-2/3 per-micro-batch traffic hides behind one micro-batch of compute.
  • Step time = (A + PP − 1) × tmicro-batch + exposed DP. Load imbalance, stragglers, kernel efficiency of small GEMMs, and hierarchical collectives are ignored. Use this to compare layouts, not to predict a real run.
