# Module 13 — Beyond dense Transformers

## Purpose

Use the bottlenecks established during the course to understand why alternative
architectures exist and what they actually change.

## Key ideas

- mixture-of-experts models;
- long-context mechanisms;
- state-space and recurrent alternatives;
- multimodal and diffusion-style language models.

## Practical task

Compare several architecture families against the course dimensions: data,
training compute, memory, communication and inference.

## Expected output

A concise architecture comparison and a justified proposal for the next
experiment or system change.

## Material to add later

- comparison matrix;
- selected paper excerpts;
- code-reading examples;
- final discussion prompts.

## References

- [Switch Transformers: Scaling to Trillion Parameter Models with Simple and Efficient Sparsity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.03961)
  — mixture-of-experts routing and capacity factors;
- [Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752)
  — selective state space models as an alternative to attention.
