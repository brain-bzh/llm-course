# Session 5 — Data selection

## Purpose

Ask which documents deserve a limited training budget and design a fair test of
the answer.

## Key ideas

- filtering and deduplication;
- quality and model-loss signals;
- diversity and domain balance;
- contamination and controlled comparison.

## Practical task

Construct random and selected subsets of the same size, then train or evaluate
them under an identical protocol.

## Expected output

A selected corpus and a controlled comparison that supports—or rejects—the
selection hypothesis.

## Material to add later

- corpus metadata;
- selection baselines;
- embedding or loss-scoring example;
- experiment-report template.
